Put this on the home screen

  1. Stay in Safari. Do not use Chrome.
  2. Tap the Share button. It is a square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. On iPhone it is at the bottom of the screen. On iPad it is at the top right.
  4. Scroll the list down. Do not swipe the coloured app icons.
  5. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  6. If you cannot see it, tap Edit Actions at the bottom of that list, turn Add to Home Screen on, then tap it.
  7. Check the name says THM V1.1.
  8. Tap Add at the top right.
  9. Leave Safari. Tap the new THM V1.1 icon. It has a yellow V1 on the house.
